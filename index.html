Why This Roadmap Exists

Stephen Krashen's input hypothesis says language acquisition happens when learners receive messages they can understand, especially input just a little beyond their current level.

The Natural Approach

This is the spirit of Manki Cantonese: watch things that are interesting, understandable, and enjoyable enough that you keep coming back.

How It Should Feel

You do not need to translate every sentence or memorize every word. Pick a video where you can follow the situation, the pictures, the gestures, and most of the meaning.

Start with the easiest section where the videos still feel interesting.
Try one or two episodes before judging a series.
Rewatch comfortable videos when you want confidence.
Move upward when you can follow the story without pausing constantly.
Use the learner paths below as examples, not rules.

I started with the storybook videos and watched them many times, especially What do you think of?, Bear, and Where's the baby. After that I added daily-life skits, vlog shorts, and student interviews for motivation. Then I moved into game/story videos like Florence, Random Game, Is this seat taken?, and Good Pizza. Path: Storybooks -> Bear -> daily-life shorts/skits -> Florence / Random Game / Is this seat taken? -> Father and Nun / Toem -> Sims later.
@DANALDTRAMP, beginner to broad channel exploration
